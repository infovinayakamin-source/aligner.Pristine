ALIGNERS OR BRACES?

Clear Aligners or Braces: Which Suits Your Smile?

Both can straighten teeth. The real difference is how they look and feel in your daily life.

Clear Aligners

  • Transparent and nearly invisible
  • Removable while eating and brushing
  • Smooth trays with no wires or brackets
  • Treatment planned with a 3D digital scan

Braces

  • Brackets and wires fixed on the teeth
  • Stay in place throughout treatment
  • No trays to remember to wear
  • Often suited to more complex cases
Not sure which is right for you? The dentist will suggest the most suitable option after examining your teeth.
SUITABILITY
